About Me

I am a graduate student studying geospatial technologies with experience in GIS, conservation planning, spatial analysis, and environmental mapping. I currently work as a conservation planner and am interested in using GIS to support natural resource management and conservation decision-making.

GIS Projects

Coastal Erosion and Accretion (2005–2025)

This project examines coastal changes near Portland, Maine, between 2005 and 2025 using GIS and remote sensing techniques. The analysis identifies potential areas of erosion and accretion along the coastline over a 20-year period. The final map highlights these changes to help visualize how coastal environments may have changed over time.

Potential Solar Farm Sites in New Hampshire

This project uses GIS to identify potential locations for solar farm development in New Hampshire. The final map highlights areas identified as suitable for solar energy development and illustrates how spatial analysis can support renewable energy planning and land-use decisions.
